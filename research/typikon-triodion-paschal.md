# Typikon: Triodion + Paschal-cycle rules (issue #8)

Parent map: #5. Source: Antiochian Typikon (Khoury/Morris 2011), Ch. VI (Triodion), Ch. VII (Pentecostarion), plus Ch. I general rules and Mar 25 section where they bear on these days. Paraphrase only; short incipits only. No PDF/long excerpts committed.

## Conventions

- **Cite**: "AT <section>, p.NN". NN = number in the "Back to Table of Contents NN" footer that closes the page. The Typikon's own TOC numbers run ~2-3 lower (e.g. TOC: Palm Sunday 502, Pascha 516; footer: 504, 518). Footer numbers used here.
- **E** explicit rule. **G** gap (Typikon silent or defers to the Triodion/Pentecostarion book). **Q** quirk/ambiguity/internal conflict. **I** = inferred (none asserted here without flag).
- "Res" = Resurrection material of Octoechos tone of the week. "Triodion" = proper material (book texts; Typikon gives only counts/slots).
- Typikon gives **slots and counts**, not hymn texts. Fits the project goal (output = list of Variable parts).
- Lenten weekday, Holy Week (other than Palm Sunday / Lazarus Sat / Holy Sat-Pascha) out of scope. Listed only where they locate in-scope days.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Triodion Sundays: Octoechos is kept as the frame, Triodion/saint material is interleaved.** Pre-Lent and Lent Sundays 1-4 keep Res stichera, Res troparion, Res kathisma, evlogetaria, hypakoe/anabathmoi/prokeimenon, eothinon Gospel + exaposteilarion, Octoechos canon. Triodion adds a fixed number of stichera/canon/kathisma slots (counts in §3). AT p.470-471, 486-495.
2. **Counts are per-Sunday, not a single formula.** Vespers "Lord I have cried" 10 = 7+3 (Pharisee/Prodigal), 6+4 (Meatfare, Cheesefare, Lent 1-4). Praises 8 or 9 (Lent 2 = 9). AT pp.470, 473, 475, 486, 489, 490, 494.
3. **Octoechos canon is dropped on Lent 3 (Cross)**; Triodion canon carries Res-flavoured first troparia. Stated in Ch. I canons rule AT p.26 and Ch. VI Lent 3, p.491. Other Triodion Sundays: both canons.
4. **Octoechos cut-off in Pascha cycle**: from Lazarus Saturday until the leavetaking of Thomas Sunday, Oktoechos not used (AT fn 290, p.503). Octoechos resumes with Myrrhbearers (Res tone 2) onward. Tones are fixed in Ch. VII for each Sunday (§6).
5. **Palm Sunday, Thomas Sunday, Pentecost: no Res stichera** (AT Ch. I, p.12). Pascha, Ascension: own services.
6. **Basil's Liturgy on Lenten Sundays: only partly explicit.** Explicit "St Basil" for Lent 1, 3, 4. Lent 2, 5, pre-Lent Sundays: Typikon silent. No general rule "Basil on Lenten Sundays" anywhere. See gaps.
7. **Pascha-cycle Sundays are fully slot-listed** (Thomas to All Saints), incl. eothinon number and Epistle/Gospel refs. Good check values for the calendar's tone/eothinon/reading data.
8. **Ch. I carries the coincidence rules** (feast on Triodion/Pascha Sundays). Pointers in §8; analysis belongs to the coincidence ticket.

## 2. General rules from Ch. I that these days rely on (E)

- Sunday Midnight Office: on Triodion Sundays (except Lent 3) the hypakoe slot is replaced by the 3 penitential troparia. AT Ch. I, p.20. (Midnight Office itself is outside the Vespers/Matins/Liturgy target.)
- Polyeleos psalms: Ps 136 on Meatfare and Cheesefare Sundays; Ps 134-135 feasts of Lord/great saints; Ps 44 Theotokos. AT p.22.
- Matins Gospel on Triodion Sundays: replace the usual post-Gospel hymns ("Through the intercessions...") with the Triodion set (Glory/Now/after-"Have mercy" idiomela). AT p.29. Position variant: NA Antiochian puts Gospel after prokeimenon, before canons; Typikon text as printed puts it after ode 8. AT fn 242 p.471, fn 19/21 pp.26,29.
- Kontakion/oikos after ode 6 come from Triodion on all Triodion Sundays **except Lent 2, 4, 5** (those use Octoechos). Sundays of Pentecostarion: Pentecostarion. AT p.28.
- Katavasia "I shall open my mouth" on Lent 2, 4, 5; Triodion katavasia on Pharisee, Prodigal, Meatfare, Cheesefare, Lent 1, 3 (after Meeting leavetaking). AT p.28-29.
- 9th ode (megalynaria in place of "More honorable"): on Sundays of Pentecostarion until Ascension use 9th ode of the feast with its megalynaria/katavasia. AT p.29.
- Praises Glory: on Triodion and Pentecostarion Sundays the Glory is the Sunday's own, not the eothinon Glory. Now = "Most blessed". On Myrrhbearers/Paralytic/Samaritan/Blind Ch. I says "Today is the day of Resurrection" (Pascha, tone 5) replaces the eothinon Glory; Ch. VII lists it as the Now with a proper/eothinon Glory (Q). AT pp.30-32; Ch. VII pp.528-533.
- "Today is salvation" (tones 1-4) / "Having risen" (tones 5-8) after Great Doxology; Palm Sunday and Thomas Sunday use the feast troparion; Lazarus Saturday uses "Today is salvation". AT p.33; fn 24 p.33; Ch. IX p.571.
- Liturgy troparia: Res first always; saint's troparion not sung on a Sunday unless the saint has a Vespers Glory. AT p.16 (Vespers) and p.35 (Liturgy).
- Trisagion replaced by "As many as have been baptized": Lazarus Saturday, Holy Saturday, Pascha, Bright Week, Pascha leavetaking, Pentecost. "Before thy Cross" on Lent 3. AT p.37.
- Communion hymn: "Praise ye the Lord" on ordinary Sundays; Lent 3 "light of thy countenance"; Sundays 1-4 after Thomas "Receive ye the Body of Christ"; All Saints "Rejoice in the Lord, O ye just". AT p.39.
- Kontakion schedule: Lent 1-5 "Champion Leader" (except Lent 5 after the Annunciation); Pascha to leavetaking: Pascha kontakion; week after Thomas: Thomas kontakion; Mid-Pentecost to leavetaking: Mid-Pentecost kontakion; Pentecost to leavetaking: Pentecost kontakion; All Saints: own. Saturdays of Souls: "Give rest". AT pp.35-37.
- "Alleluia" after Epistle on every Liturgy; replaced only on Holy Saturday. AT p.38.
- Great Prokeimenon at Sat Vespers: Triodion's own from Cheesefare Sunday eve and Lent Sundays; Pentecostarion's during Bright Week; otherwise "The Lord is king". AT p.13.
- Great Vespers aposticha: Sunday Res aposticha is never dropped unless the whole Res service is omitted. From Thomas to Sunday before Ascension, the aposticha = first Res sticheron + the Paschal stichera ("Let God arise..." with Pascha doxastikon). AT p.14.

## 3. Triodion Sundays (E unless marked)

### 3.1 Pharisee and Publican (AT pp.470-471)
- **Sat Vespers**: Lord-I-cried 10 = 7 Res + 3 Triodion; Glory Triodion; Now Res. Aposticha: Res stichera; Glory Triodion; Now Theotokos (tone). Troparia: Res apolytikion; theotokion.
- **Matins**: God-is-the-Lord: Res troparion 2x + theotokion 1x. Res kathisma; evlogetaria; hypakoe/anabathmoi/prokeimenon of tone. Canons: Res + Triodion (verse "Glory to thee our God"). After ode 3: kontakion+oikos Res; kathisma Triodion + theotokion. After ode 6: kontakion+oikos Triodion; monologion. Katavasia Triodion. Eothinon Gospel then the Triodion hymn set (fn 242 position variant). Exaposteilaria: eothinon, Triodion, Theotokos. Praises 8 = 5 Res + 3 Triodion; Glory Triodion; Now "Most blessed". Great Doxology.
- **Liturgy**: Typika; Beatitudes 8 = 4 Res + 4 from Triodion canon ode 6. Little Entrance troparia: Res, patron, Triodion kontakion. Epistle/Gospel "from the Triodion" (refs not given). "It is meet" at Especially; communion "Praise ye the Lord"; "We have seen".
- "We follow this order every Sunday while we use the Triodion" (p.471, on the Gospel/Triodion-hymn block).
- Liturgy identity (Chrysostom/Basil): not stated. G.

### 3.2 Prodigal Son (AT p.471)
- E: "order as found in the Sunday of the Pharisee and the Publican". No deltas. Triodion texts differ but slots identical.

### 3.3 Meatfare Saturday of Souls (AT pp.471-473)
- **Fri-evening Vespers** (the service the Typikon gives; no separate Sat Great Vespers): Lord-I-cried 6 = 3 martyrs (tone) + 3 departed (Triodion); Glory departed; Now tone. Prokeimenon "Alleluia" tone 8 + 2 verses. Aposticha: 3 martyrs stichera, Glory/Now Triodion. After Simeon: canon for the departed (martyrs' troparia + verses), Trisagion for the Dead.
- **Matins Sat**: Alleluia replaces God-is-the-Lord; departed troparia; Ps with kathisma/martyrikon for departed; evlogetaria for departed; Triodion canon (kathisma after 3, kontakion/oikos after 6); aposticha in tone; Glory/Now Triodion.
- **Liturgy**: Typika; Beatitudes from Triodion canon odes 3 and 6; kontakion for departed; communion "Blessed are those whom thou hast chosen"; after "Blessed be the name" Trisagion for the Departed.
- fn 248 p.473: if a Great Feast falls on this day, use the feast's order. Defers; G for the combined order.

### 3.4 Meatfare Sunday (AT pp.473-474)
- **Sat Vespers**: 10 = 6 Res + 4 Triodion. "Rest of the service for Meatfare Sunday in the Triodion". G for aposticha/troparia/prokeimenon (the Great Prokeimenon is the Triodion's per Ch. I p.13).
- Sunday morning (Midnight Office): Glory = Triodion litia; Now Theotokos; penitential troparia.
- **Matins/Liturgy**: no explicit listing; apply Pharisee order (p.471 statement). G/inferred.
- Sunday-evening Vespers: aposticha Triodion. (Not Sat Vespers.)
- Polyeleos Ps 136 (Ch. I p.22).

### 3.5 Cheesefare Sunday (AT p.475)
- **Sat Vespers**: 10 = 6 Res + 4 Triodion; rest per Triodion. G.
- Midnight Office: as Meatfare. Matins/Liturgy unspecified (Pharisee order). G.
- Forgiveness Vespers (Sunday eve): 10 = 4 repentance (tone) + 3 day + 3 Menaion; Great Prokeimenon; 4 troparia + 40 Lord-have-mercy etc. Template for Sunday-evening Lenten Vespers (AT fn 252 p.476: followed on all Lent Sundays). Not Sat Vespers.

### 3.6 Lent 1, Orthodoxy (AT pp.486-488)
- **Sat Vespers**: 10 = 6 Res + 4 Triodion; Glory Triodion; Now Res. Aposticha: Res; Glory/Now Triodion. Troparia: Res, Orthodoxy troparion, theotokion tone 2.
- **Matins**: troparia as Vespers. Canons Res + Triodion. After ode 3 kontakion/oikos Res + kathisma (Triodion, Theotokos). After ode 6 kontakion/oikos Triodion + monologia (Menaion and Triodion). Katavasia Triodion. Eothinon Gospel + Triodion hymns. Exaposteilaria: eothinon, Triodion, Theotokos. Praises 8 = 4 Res + 4 Triodion; Glory Triodion; Now "Most blessed". Great Doxology.
- **Liturgy**: **three proper antiphons** (texts from Farrow, not reproduced) instead of Typika; antiphon 3 interleaves Orthodoxy troparion. Little Entrance: Res, Orthodoxy troparion, patron; kontakion "Champion Leader". Epistle/Gospel Orthodoxy. **"The rest of the Divine Liturgy of St. Basil"**. End prayer is "O Lord who blessest" (fn 278: Basil's closing thanksgiving only Jan 1/Athos).
- Icon procession after Great Doxology (fn 276; NA practice: end of Liturgy). Patriarchal Trisagion for the Dead after dismissal. Optional practice; not Variable parts.
- Sun-evening: Lenten Daily Vespers as Cheesefare; Great Prokeimenon proper.

### 3.7 Lent 2, Gregory Palamas (AT pp.489-490)
- **Sat Vespers**: 10 = 6 Res + 4 Palamas; Glory Palamas; Now Res. Aposticha Res; Glory Palamas (Now not stated: Q). Troparia: Res, Palamas, theotokion tone 8.
- **Matins**: canons Res + Palamas. After ode 3: kontakion+kathisma Palamas. After ode 6: kontakion+oikos **Res**. Katavasia "I shall open my mouth". Eothinon Gospel + Triodion hymns. Exaposteilaria: eothinon, Palamas, Theotokos. **Praises 9** = 5 Res + 4 Triodion (3 Palamas + 1 Lent); Glory Lenten idiomelon; Now "Most blessed".
- **Liturgy**: Typika; Beatitudes 4 Res + 4 ode 6 Palamas; kontakion "Champion Leader"; "rest of the service for the Sunday" (Liturgy identity G).

### 3.8 Lent 3, Cross (AT pp.490-493)
- **Sat Vespers**: 10 = 6 Res + 4 Cross; Glory Cross; Now Theotokos (tone). Aposticha Res; Glory/Now Cross. Troparia: Res, Cross, theotokion tone 1 (Q: text prints the Cross troparion incipit under "theotokion").
- **Matins**: troparia as Vespers. Polyeleos. 1st+2nd Res kathisma, evlogetaria, Cross kathisma, hypakoe/anabathmoi Res. **Canon: Triodion only** (Octoechos canon omitted; "Glory to thy Holy Resurrection" after first troparion of each ode, "Glory to thee our God" between rest). After ode 3 Triodion kathisma; after ode 6 Triodion kontakion/oikos; both monologia. **Katavasia Cross**. Eothinon Gospel + Triodion hymns. Exaposteilaria: Res, Cross, Theotokos. Praises 8 = 4 Res + 4 Cross; Glory Triodion; Now "Most blessed". Procession and veneration of the Cross after Great Doxology.
- **Liturgy**: Cross antiphons (proper psalm verses; 3rd antiphon with Cross troparion). Little Entrance: Res, Cross, patron; kontakion "Champion Leader". "Before thy Cross" instead of Trisagion. **Basil.** Communion "light of thy countenance". Instead of "We have seen": Cross troparion.
- Typika/Beatitudes not mentioned on Lent 1 and 3; antiphons given instead. Conflicts with Ch. I "every Sunday Typika" (p.34). Resolve by specificity (Ch. VI wins). Flag Q.
- Mon/Wed/Fri after: "Before thy Cross" at Vespers (not scope).

### 3.9 Lent 4, John of the Ladder (AT pp.494-495)
- **Sat Vespers**: 10 = 6 Res + 4 John; Glory John; Now Res. Aposticha Res; Glory John; Now Theotokos tone 2. Troparia: Res, John, theotokion tone 8.
- **Matins**: "rest of Sunday Matins ... from the Oktoechos". Canons Res + Triodion. After ode 3: kontakion+kathisma John. After ode 6: kontakion/oikos Res. Katavasia "I shall open my mouth". Eothinon Gospel + Triodion hymns. Exaposteilaria: eothinon, John, Theotokos. Praises 8 = 4 Res + 4 from John's Vespers (with verses); Glory Triodion; Now "Most blessed".
- **Liturgy**: Typika; Beatitudes 4 Res + 4 ode 6 first Triodion canon; kontakion "Champion Leader"; **Basil**.

### 3.10 Lent 5, Mary of Egypt (AT p.502)
- **Sat Vespers**: "order for Vespers and Matins from the Triodion". **G**: no counts.
- **Matins**: canons Res + two Triodion canons (verses given). Exaposteilaria: eothinon, Mary of Egypt, Theotokos. Praises 8 = 4 Res + 4 Mary (from Vespers, with verses); Glory Triodion ("Kingdom of God"); Now "Most blessed". Great Doxology.
- **Liturgy**: only kontakion given: "Champion Leader" if Annunciation not yet passed; "O undisputed Intercessor" after it (Ch. I p.36 words it as "if Annunciation falls before the Saturday of the Akathist"; same intent). Rest G.
- Akathist Saturday (AT pp.498-501): Matins + Chrysostom Liturgy given (Beatitudes ode 3+6 Theotokos canon; "With mystic apprehension"; Theotokos Epistle/Gospel; communion "I will receive the cup of salvation"). Friday-evening Akathist is Compline.
- Sunday evening: Cheesefare model + sermon.

### 3.11 Saturdays of Lent (AT pp.485, 488-489, 494, 498-501)
- **Sat 1 (Theodore of Tyre)**: Matins + Liturgy only (no Sat Vespers); Beatitudes: 4 from ode 3 canon 1 + 4 from ode 6 canon 2; Epistle for saint, Gospel of the day; koliva blessing after dismissal. p.485.
- **Sat 2** (3 and 4 identical, fn 281 p.489; Sun 2/3/4 "Saturday morning as 2nd Saturday"): Matins: Alleluia/"O Holy Apostles and Martyrs" instead of God-is-the-Lord; departed kathisma/martyrikon, two parts of the "Blessed are they" psalm as on Souls Saturday, evlogetaria for the departed; canon of saint of the day alone to ode 5, then Triodion ode after each ode; exaposteilaria departed + Theotokos; Liturgy: daily antiphons; kontakion = the departed troparion "O thou who with wisdom..."; Especially "It is truly meet"; communion "Blessed ...chosen"; petition for departed before dismissal. pp.488-489.
- Mar 25 Annunciation on Akathist Saturday: AT p.300 (not analyzed).

## 4. Lazarus Saturday and Palm Sunday (AT pp.503-506)

### 4.1 Lazarus Saturday (p.503-504)
- **Vespers**: no Sat Great Vespers given. Fri 5th week Vespers+Presanctified 8 stichera: 3 Triodion (repeat first) + 5 Lazarus; Glory Lazarus; Now Triodion (p.502). G: the evening Vespers of Lazarus Sat is Palm Sunday's Great Vespers (§4.2).
- **Matins**: troparion "O Christ God, when thou didst raise Lazarus" 3x after 6 Psalms. Kathisma Triodion 2x; **evlogetaria for the Resurrection**; second kathismata 2x; "In that we have beheld"; Ps 50. Two Triodion canons ("Glory to thee our God"); after ode 3 kathisma; after ode 5 the Triodion ode 6; kontakion/oikos/monologia; katavasia Triodion; 9th ode of both canons (no "Exalt ye"). Exaposteilarion Lazarus Sat. Praises 8 Triodion; Glory "fulfilled"; Now "Most blessed". Great Doxology; "Today is salvation" (Ch. I p.33).
- **Liturgy: Chrysostom.** Typika; Beatitudes odes 3+6 second canon; entrance hymn "Come let us worship...who rose from the dead"; Lazarus troparion + kontakion; "As many as have been baptized"; Especially: 9th ode heirmos of canon 1; communion "Out of the mouth of infants"; Lazarus troparion for "We have seen"; dismissal with "who rose from the dead".
- fn 290 p.503: **no Octoechos from here to Thomas leavetaking**. fn 291 p.504 (attached to Lazarus Sat dismissal): koliva memorial services not permitted on Palm Sunday and Thomas Sunday (extreme need: 4 troparia for the dead after Liturgy); fish allowed Palm Sunday.

### 4.2 Palm Sunday (p.504-506)
- **Great Vespers** (day not stated; heading only "At Great Vespers": Q, presumably Sat eve, adjacent to Midnight Office/Matins "on Sunday"): Sunset Psalm; whole kathisma "Blessed is the man"; Lord-I-cried 10 = 5 stichera tone 6 sung twice; Glory/Now repeat sticheron 1; entrance; OT readings; aposticha Triodion; troparia: Lazarus (2x), "buried with thee" (1x). No Res stichera (Ch. I p.12).
- **Matins**: troparia as Vespers; polyeleos; kathisma; **evlogetaria not sung**; antiphon 1 tone 4 anabathmoi; prokeimenon "Out of the mouths of infants"; Matins Gospel; no "In that we have beheld"; Ps 50 + Triodion hymns. Canon Triodion only (no verse except Glory/Now); after ode 3 hypakoe; after ode 6 kontakion/oikos + monologia (Menaion + Sunday); katavasia Palm Sunday; entire 9th ode (no "More honorable"); **no exaposteilaria**, only "Holy is the Lord" 3x; palm blessing then; praises with Palm Sunday idiomelon; Great Doxology; troparion "buried with thee".
- **Liturgy: Chrysostom.** Proper antiphons; entrance hymn proper; troparia: Lazarus, "buried with thee"; kontakion "Upborne"; Trisagion kept ("Holy God" listed); Palm Sunday Epistle/Gospel; Especially: ode 9 heirmos; communion "Blessed is he who comes".
- Palm Sunday evening (Bridegroom/Compline) is Holy Week; not scope.
- Ch. I p.11: Palm Sunday and Thomas Sunday: kathisma recited and Res service omitted.

## 5. Holy Saturday and Pascha (AT pp.514-523)

- **Holy Saturday Vespers + Liturgy of Basil** = Pascha's preceding Vespers (pp.516-517). Lord-I-cried 8 = 4 Res tone 1 + 4 idiomela for Holy Saturday; Glory/Now "Moses". Entrance with Gospel. 15 OT readings (list in fn 317 p.517; 3 read at body text); special prokeimenon "Arise O God"; Basil; "Let all mortal flesh"; Gospel Matthew "After the Sabbath"; "As many as have been baptized"; communion hymn; "Remember us O merciful One"; dismissal "who rose from the dead". fn 312 p.514-515: modern parishes do Matins at the first hour after sunset, Vespers+Liturgy Sat morning (variant).
- **Pascha** (pp.518-522):
  - Midnight Office, Rush procession, Matins with Paschal canon tone 1 (hypakoe after ode 3; kontakion/oikos/synaxarion after ode 6; megalynaria with ode 9), exaposteilarion "When thou didst fall asleep" 3x, praises 4 Res tone 1 + Paschal verses, Glory/Now "Today is the Day".
  - **Liturgy: Chrysostom.** Paschal antiphons; Little Entrance antiphon 3 "Christ is risen"; entrance hymn "In the congregations"; troparion, hypakoe tone 4, kontakion tone 8; "As many as have been baptized"; Epistle Acts 1:1-8; Gospel John 1:1-17; megalynarion "The angel spake to her"; communion "Receive ye the Body of Christ"; "Christ is risen" replaces "We have seen"/"Blessed be the name"; Paschal dialogue instead of "Through the prayers".
  - Agape Vespers (Pascha Sunday evening): Lord-I-cried 6 = Res tone 2 + Glory tone 2; entrance with Gospel; Gospel John 20:19-25; aposticha Res + Paschal stichera. Optional/practice-bound.
- **Paschal Office** replaces Hours/Compline/Midnight until Saturday before Thomas and at leavetaking (p.523).
- Season-wide: no "O Heavenly King" until Pentecost; "Christ is risen" 3x for "Holy God" at Matins, "O come let us worship" at Vespers, "We have seen" at Liturgy until leavetaking of Pascha. p.524. Fasting: none Wed/Fri until Ascension (p.524).
- Bright Week Mon: Pascha Matins pattern; Chrysostom; Epistle Acts 1:12-17,23-26, Gospel John 1:18-28 (p.524). Friday (Life-Giving Fountain) Mon-Fri slot data pp.524-526. Out of scope except as locator.

## 10. Encoding implications (short)
