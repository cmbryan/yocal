# Lent Sunday-eve Great Vespers prokeimenon: Typikon vs ANCA

Issue: cmbryan/yocal#23 (map #5; follows #21 gap 3). Researched 2026-11-05. Research branch, throwaway, never merged.
Public repo: nothing copied. Paraphrase + short incipits only. No PDFs committed.

Status tags: **E** explicit, **I** inferred, **M** missing.
Cites: `Typ Ch I §3 p.13` = Antiochian Typikon (Khoury/Morris 2011) printed page (footer "Back to Table of Contents N" ends page N). `Typ Ch VI p.N` = Triodion chapter. `Wichita` = Bp Basil Parish Typikon Charts / Descriptions of Ranks (dowama.org). ANCA = antiochian.org `servicetexts/<Mon DD YYYY> {VESP,Abbr}.pdf`.

## TL;DR

- **Conflict is not real.** The Typikon sentence (Ch I §3 p.13) says "eves of the Sundays of the Triodion from Cheese Fare Sunday and the Sundays of Great Lent" use a Great Prokeimenon "found in the Triodion". Ch VI gives that Great Prokeimenon at **Sunday-evening Lenten Vespers** (Cheesefare Sunday evening p.475: "Turn not away thy face..."; Lent 1 Sunday evening p.488: "Thou hast given an inheritance..."), not at Saturday Great Vespers. "Eve of the Sunday" in the p.13 sentence = Sunday evening Vespers (I, strong).
- **Saturday-evening Great Vespers prokeimenon on Triodion Sundays = "The Lord is King" (tone 6).** E for the default rule (Typ Ch I §3 p.13 bullet 1, no Triodion exception listed there; Ch VI Saturday-evening entries for Cheesefare, Lent 1-4 list no prokeimenon override; Lent 5 refers to Triodion without slot list). Confirmed by ANCA 56/56 Saturday eves (2019-2026: Meatfare, Cheesefare, Lent 1-5).
- **Recommendation:** Typikon and ANCA agree once p.13 is read as Sunday evening. Rule: Triodion Sat Great Vespers = "Lord is King" (E+key). Do not encode the Great Prokeimenon at Saturday Vespers. Sunday-evening Lenten Vespers is out of scope for map (Lenten weekday services); record the Great Prokeimenon texts as a note only.
- **No ANCA/UK divergence found.** UK practice unknown (M).
- Residual: p.13 wording is genuinely ambiguous (I), Triodion book not read (M), Lent 2-5 Sunday-evening prokeimenon text not stated in Typikon (M), no answer key for Sunday-evening Vespers (ANCA does not publish it) (M).

Why "eves" = Sunday evening, not Saturday evening (I):
1. The phrase starts "from Cheese Fare Sunday". Great Prokeimenon at Cheesefare Sunday *evening* is stated (p.475); no Saturday-evening Triodion entry states any prokeimenon.
2. Liturgical usage "eve" here fits Sunday-evening Vespers = eve of the following Lenten week; the Typikon elsewhere distinguishes "Great Vespers on Saturday evening" vs "Vespers on Sunday evening" in the same chapter.
3. Ch VI Saturday entries are otherwise complete slot lists (stichera counts, aposticha, troparia); a Great Prokeimenon at Great Vespers would be unusual to omit.
Counter-reading (Saturday eve Great Prokeimenon): fits only the bare p.13 sentence; no Ch VI support; ANCA contradicts it 56/56. Rejected.

## 2. Wichita charts

- Chart II (Sundays) Great Vespers prokeimenon row: "The Lord is King" for 5th-2nd class Sunday columns, "of the day" for Master-feast column. No Triodion column, no Triodion footnote on prokeimenon. E for default, silent on Triodion.
- Descriptions of Ranks fn 5: Great Vespers with Great Prokeimenon is Master-feast eve and Thomas Sunday; Palm Sunday evening is Daily Vespers with Sunday-evening prokeimenon "Behold now bless ye". Mentions "the preceding Sunday evenings of Great Lent" as the model for Daily Vespers with the lenten troparia: consistent with Sunday-evening Lenten Vespers being a separate, lower-rank service. E.
- Nothing in charts says Triodion Sat eve differs. Wichita = no conflict.

## 3. ANCA key (Saturday-eve Great Vespers, VESP.pdf)

Sampled all Saturday eves of Meatfare, Cheesefare, Lent 1-5 for 2019-2026 (Easter 2019 Apr 28, 2020 Apr 19, 2021 May 2, 2022 Apr 24, 2023 Apr 16, 2024 May 5, 2025 Apr 20, 2026 Apr 12). 8 years x 7 eves = **56 VESP files, 56/56 print the heading "Saturday Prokeimenon in Tone Six" with "The Lord is King" (once) + two verses**; deacon: "The evening prokeimenon!". No other prokeimenon in any file. (Pharisee/Publican eve: Jan 31 2026 already "Lord is King" in #21.)

Abbr rubrics checked 2026 Sundays Cheesefare (Feb 22), Lent 1 (Mar 1), Lent 2-5 (Mar 8, 15, 22, 29): each says Great Vespers on the Saturday is followed by "the Prokeimenon 'The Lord is King ...' in Tone 6 with its stichoi". Includes Lent 4 (Mar 22 2026; Annunciation Mar 25 not on weekend, no coincidence). E.

Dates (Saturday eve): 2026 Feb 14, 21, 28, Mar 7, 14, 21, 28; other years as listed by Easter dates above (files `Mon DD YYYY VESP.pdf`, all HTTP 200).

Not in ANCA: Sunday-evening (Forgiveness / Lenten) Vespers. `servicetexts` suffix guesses (VESP2, Forgiveness, FORGIVENESS, SUNVESP, Vesp, Vespers) for Feb 22 2026 all 404; only VESP (Sat), ORTHROS, READ, Abbr, bilingual exist. Did not render liturgicday page for Feb 22 (not tried). Sunday-evening Great Prokeimenon therefore has **no ANCA key** (M).

DCS: pre-Nov 2026 pages 404 (see #21), no Lent samples. Not usable.
