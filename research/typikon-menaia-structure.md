# Typikon Ch. V (Menaia): structure + Sunday-coincidence patterns

Issue #7 (map #5). Source: Antiochian Typikon (Khoury/Morris, 2011), Ch. V. Paraphrased; short incipits only (copyright, public repo).

## 0. Conventions

- Cite: "Typikon, <date>, p.NN". NN = printed page number in footer "Back to Table of Contents NN" (page on which the cited text sits). PDF page = NN + 3.
- Page-number caveat: TOC lists Ch. V "Sep 57 ... Aug 441"; actual footers: Ch. V title p.58, Sep 1 p.59, Nov 1 p.100, Aug 31 p.467-469, Triodion title p.470. TOC runs ~2 low. Issue's "Nov 1 p.99": Nov 1 starts on p.100 (p.99 is Oct 26 Liturgy end).
- R = Resurrection (tone of week). W = Saturday/weekday case; S = Sunday case. "Men" = pointer only ("follow the Menaion"). "chg" = Menaion + listed changes. "full" = full order given.
- Status tags: **EXPLICIT** (text states it), **INFERRED** (generalised from several per-date entries; Typikon does not state the general rule), **GAP**.
- Outside Ch. V: Ch. III (Sunday order, pp.48-53) consulted only for the generic template; Ch. IV (p.54-57), Ch. VI/VII (Triodion/Pentecostarion) not analysed.

## 1. Chapter structure

- Extent: p.58 (title) to p.469; Menaion proper p.59-469. Scope sentence (p.58): Great Vespers, Matins, Divine Liturgy for feasts of the Lord, Theotokos, great saints through the year.
- Ordering: calendar order Sep 1 -> Aug 31 (Church year). Month banners seen: Sep p.59, Oct p.93, Nov p.100, Jan p.164, Mar p.259, Apr p.325, Jun p.417, Jul p.423, Aug p.443 (Dec, Feb, May banners absent from extraction; Dec 4 on p.120, Feb 1 on p.216, May 2 on p.358). Movable-by-range Sundays interleaved at their date range (e.g. "Sunday between Oct 1-17", p.93).
- Only ~90 date/range entries (table 3a), NOT all 365 dates. Dates absent = no Ch. V order; fall back to Menaion/Ch. III (see Gaps).
- Entry anatomy (repeats per date):
  1. Heading (date + commemoration(s)).
  2. Condition line(s): "If the feast falls on Saturday or a week day, we follow the order in the Menaion." then "If ... Sunday, we follow this order."
  3. Sunday order = four blocks: **Great Vespers** (eve = Saturday evening), **Midnight Service**, **Matins**, **Divine Liturgy**. No Hours, no Small Vespers, no Compline (except pointers: Jul 5 p.426, Jul 7 p.428, Aug 5 p.448 saint's service "said at Compline").
  4. Footnotes: editorial notes (variants from 1545 Venetian Typikon, Constantinople Typikon, Greek 1881; North-American practice).
- Heading language is Julian/Pentecost-counted ("29th Sunday after Pentecost", Dec Forefathers Sunday p.126; Epistle/Gospel cycle names such as "Sunday before/after Elevation"). Typikon assumes Julian date for Pentecostarion collisions (p.325 fn 209, p.384).
- Each order lists variable parts by slot, with counts. Slots seen (Sunday Matins): troparia at "God is the Lord"; poetic kathisma pattern; evlogetaria (sung / not sung); hypakoe, anabathmoi, prokeimenon; Matins Gospel; canons (+which); kathisma after 3rd ode; kontakion/oikos after 6th; katavasia (which); "More honorable" or 9th-ode megalynaria; exaposteilaria (list); praises (count split); doxology. Liturgy: Typika/Beatitudes split; troparia after Little Entrance (order); kontakion; Epistle; Gospel; "Especially" hymn; communion hymn; dismissal phrase.

## 4. Patterns that could be generalised into Rules

All **INFERRED** unless noted. Evidence = per-date S entries. Ch. III (p.48) states the base template **EXPLICIT**: saint without "Glory" -> 7R+3 saint + "Glory/Now" tone-of-week; saint with "Glory" -> 6R+4 saint + Glory saint + Now R.

- **P1 Ordinary saint on Sunday (R priority)**. Vespers 6R+4 saint, Glory saint, Now R theotokion. Aposticha: R stichera, Glory saint, Now theotokion. Troparia: R apolytikion, saint, (theotokion). Matins: troparia as Vespers; poetic kathisma R; evlogetaria sung; hypakoe/anabathmoi/prokeimenon R; canons R + saint; saint's kathisma after ode 3; R kontakion/oikos after ode 6 (+ monologion); eothinon Gospel; exaposteilaria eothinon + saint + Theotokos; praises 4R+4 saint, Glory eothinon, Now "Most blessed"; Liturgy Typika, Beatitudes 4R+4 (6th ode of saint's canon), troparia R/saint/temple patron/kontakion, "Praise ye the Lord", "We have seen". Evidence: Nov 1 p.100; Sep 23 p.85; Oct 18 p.94; Jul 27 p.440; Aug 31 p.467 (30+ dates, table 3a).
- **P2 Two commemorations (4R+3+3)**. Second saint/feast shares 3. Dates: Sep 1 (variant), 13, 16, 20; Nov 25; Dec 4, 12, 15, 27; Jan 7, 11-30 (Triodion variant 4R+3T+3); Feb 1; Jul 17; Aug 16, 29 (Sat). Glory goes to one named party (varies: Sep 16 saint; Sep 13 Consecration; Dec 12 Forefathers).
- **P3 Higher feast (4R+6)**. Archangels Nov 8; Nicholas Dec 6; John Theologian Sep 26; Forerunner Jun 24, Aug 29; Peter & Paul Jun 29; Theotokos feasts Sep 8, Nov 21; leavetakings (Sep 12/21, Aug 13/23). No Typikon label for the class: **GAP** (6+4 vs 4+6 is per-date only; not explained by Glory presence).
- **P4 7R+3**: Jun 30 p.421; Jan 5 Sunday-before-Theophany p.168; Jan 6 Monday-case Sat Vespers p.175. Consistent with Ch. III saint-without-Glory rule (p.48).
- **P5 Feast replaces Sunday (no R)**: Cross Sep 14 p.75; Nativity p.143; Theophany p.174; Transfiguration p.449. Typical markers: Vespers "Blessed is the man" omitted (Aug 6 p.449), "In that we have beheld the Resurrection" omitted (Aug 6 p.449), canons "without verses", feast 9th ode in place of "More honorable". Not stated as a general rule. Contrast: Nativity Theotokos, Entry, Presentation, Circumcision, Dormition keep R beside the feast.
- **P6 Great-feast Sunday Matins differs from P1**: polyeleos; evlogetaria **not sung**; feast prokeimenon + Gospel with R ("In that we have beheld") after; Psalm 50 hymns; Res troparion 1x + feast 2x; 9th ode of both canons in place of "More honorable". Evidence: Sep 8 p.67; Nov 21 p.113; Feb 2 p.219; Aug 15 p.457. Contrast Jan 1 Sunday p.165: polyeleos with evlogetaria sung (inconsistency).
- **P7 Afterfeast Sunday**: 6R+4 from the Menaion's daily afterfeast stichera; Glory from litia; Matins 4R+4; Liturgy Epistle/Gospel Sunday. Evidence: p.70, p.79, p.114, p.223, p.225 (Triodion variant 4R+3T+3), p.450, p.461.
- **P8 Forefeast on Sunday**: P1 split with forefeast in saint slot (Sep 7, Nov 20, Aug 5, Aug 14); Midnight troparion of forefeast; Epistle/Gospel of Sunday (Cross-proximity cycle).
- **P9 Leavetaking on weekday**: sung as feast day minus readings/litia/polyeleos/Matins Gospel; daily Epistle/Gospel. **EXPLICIT** Sep 21 p.83; Aug 13 p.452; Aug 23 p.463; Dec 31 p.161.
- **P10 Epistle/Gospel assignment (Sunday + saint)**: default Epistle = saint, Gospel = Sunday (Nov 1, Nov 8, Nov 14, Dec 5, Jan 17-30, Jul 1-Aug 2, Aug 31). Variants: both = Sunday (Sep 6 p.63; Oct 23 p.97; Nov 20 p.111); both = saint (Nov 16 p.109; Nov 30 p.119; Nov 13 p.107; Sep 26 p.92; Jun 24/29/30 pp.417-421); Epistle/Gospel named by proximity to Cross ("Sunday before Elevation" Sep 7/8/9-afterfeast/12/13; "Sunday after Elevation" Sep 9/16/20/21); Forefathers Sunday: Epistle of the 29th Sunday after Pentecost, Gospel Forefathers p.128; Dec 12/15 Epistle saint + Gospel Forefathers pp.129-130. No single rule: per date.
- **P11 Seasonal katavasia / kontakion**: from first Sunday or saint's feast after Cross leavetaking, Theotokos katavasia until Nov 21 and kontakion "O Undisputed Intercessor" until Nov 8 (fn 54 p.85) **EXPLICIT**; Entry katavasia/kontakion from Nov 8 (fn 64 p.101); Cross katavasia in Sep entries (e.g. p.61) and Aug 31 (p.467); Nativity katavasia from Nov 21 (p.114); Epiphany katavasia Jan 1 (p.165); Transfiguration katavasia Aug 5 (p.449). Boundary dates between seasons mostly implicit **GAP**.
- **P12 Midnight Service on Sunday**: triadikos canon + (litia if saint has service) + troparion; "whole service for the Sunday" when no feast (Nov 1 p.100, Nov 11 p.103, Nov 12 p.104). Matches Ch. III p.49.
- **P13 Sat-eve Great Vespers = first block of S order** (Vespers headed "Saturday evening" in Jun-Aug entries p.417+).
- **P14 Triodion substitution**: where Sunday is in Triodion, 3 stichera "from the Triodion" replace 1 (or 3) slots; Glory from Triodion; Meatfare variants. Evidence: Jan 11-Feb 10 pp.183-236.

## 6. Gaps (what Ch. V leaves undefined)

1. **Menaion pointer content (T1)**: >=37 "Saturday/weekday: follow Menaion" statements. Their content lies in the HTM Menaion (fn 41 p.62), not provided here. **GAP** for weekday services (out of map scope except Saturdays).
2. **Saturday feast (eve Vespers of Sunday)**: explicit Saturday orders for only Oct 26, Jun 24, Aug 29, Dec 26. For all other dates, a feast on Saturday = "Menaion" with no statement on how Saturday Matins/Liturgy combine with the tone-of-week Saturday service (Octoechos) or on Saturday evening Vespers (which is Sunday's). Only readings swaps (T5) given for Sep 12/13/16, Dec 27, Jan 11, Jan 14.
3. **Eve-of-feast Vespers when feast is Monday**: stated only for Dec 25 (p.145), Jan 6 (p.174), Mar 25 (p.314), Aug 2 (p.447 Sunday evening Great Paraklesis), Apr 23/25. Generic Monday feast: **GAP**.
4. **Great feast Saturday/weekday not stated**: Sep 14 (single order), Aug 6 (Sunday only; weekday = Menaion implicit), Jan 6 (Sat/Sun/Mon full; ok), Feb 2 (changes only; Menaion).
5. **Rank/class**: no label for why 6R+4 vs 4R+6 vs 4R+3+3; per-date only. Great-saint threshold unstated (Ch. III says "great saint/greater importance" p.49-50 without list). Generalisation required = INFERRED only.
6. **Dates with no Ch. V order** (~270 of 365): fallback = Ch. III p.48-50 generic template (7+3 / 6+4 rule) + Menaion. Complement list not computed here.
7. **Litia / Artoklasia**: Ch. V lists "litia" only as a Midnight-Service slot or "Glory" source; no Great Vespers litia/artoklasia/blessing of loaves structure (only Ch. IV p.54-57 vigil template; one artoklasia mention May 21 p.395). **GAP** for Vigil structure (Not-yet-specified item in map #5).
8. **Resurrection-vs-feast precedence generalisation**: P5 vs P6 not stated as a precedence rule; only per-date (Cross, Transfiguration, Nativity, Theophany drop R). Hierarchy for non-Twelve (e.g. Peter & Paul, Forerunner, Archangels) is 4R+6, i.e. R kept.
9. **Movable feasts**: Palm Sunday/Ascension/Pentecost orders not in Ch. V (Ch. VI/VII); only saint-coincidence cases (Mar 25, May 2/8/21/25).
10. **Calendar assumption**: Julian. Typikon applies Pentecostarion branches by Julian date; New-calendar collisions (e.g. St George before Pascha) handled only by editor notes p.325 fn 209.
11. **Triodion/Pentecostarion Sunday + Menaion saints** for dates absent from the list: no rule (only the ~12 dates with grids).
12. **Counts omitted** in some S orders (e.g. Aug 15 praises) - need inference from Menaion.
