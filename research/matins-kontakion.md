# Sunday Matins kontakion: resurrectional vs feast (issue #27)

Question: at Sunday Matins (6th-ode slot), when is the Octoechos resurrectional kontakion+oikos replaced by the feast's, incl. Theotokos / Master (Lord) feast coincidence, Triodion, Pentecostarion? Check against ANCA ORTHROS / Abbr.

Labels: **explicit** = source states it; **inferred** = from ANCA key (evidence given); **missing** = no source, no key.
Paraphrase only; no source/key text committed. Typikon = Antiochian Typikon (Khoury/Morris 2011), printed page numbers. Violakis = 1888 Typikon, user's translation `Violakis/translation.md` (section, printed page).

## Answer

Slot = "kontakion and oikos after the 6th ode" (ANCA: one slot "Kontakion, Oikos and Synaxarion", after the Intercession; Abbr rubric 17).

1. **Default: resurrectional** Octoechos kontakion+oikos of the tone. Explicit (Typikon p.28 s.X; Violakis §26 p.21).
2. **Replaced by the feast's/Sunday's kontakion+oikos** when any of:
   - A Theotokos feast falls on the Sunday (also Theotokos leavetaking, Ch.V). Explicit (p.28; Violakis §26).
   - Triodion Sundays **except Lent 2, 4, 5**. Explicit (p.28; §26).
   - Pentecostarion Sundays (Thomas ... All Saints). Explicit (p.28; §26). Ch.V overrides, see conflict C2.
   - Sunday of Forefathers, Genealogy (Sunday before Nativity), Sunday after Nativity, 3 Sundays of the Fathers (1st/4th/7th Councils), All Saints. Explicit (p.28; §26 phrases it "Sunday before Nativity and Sunday after").
   - Listed saints on a Sunday: Nativity of Forerunner, SS Peter & Paul, John Evangelist (Sep 26), Andrew, Three Hierarchs (only before the Triodion), Archangels, Demetrius, Nicholas. Explicit (p.28; §26). Ch.V adds more, see "Ch.V extensions".
   - Pentecostarion saints George (Apr 23), John (May 8), Constantine & Helen (May 21): feast's. Explicit p.28, but Ch.V narrows, see C2.
3. **Where the resurrectional kontakion goes when displaced** (Theotokos feast, listed saints, great saints, Triodion): read after the **3rd ode**, before the feast kathisma. Explicit: Typikon p.27 s.IX, Ch.V per date (e.g. Sep 8 p.67, Nov 21 p.114, Nov 13 p.107); Violakis §25 p.20, §26 p.21. Not stated for Pentecostarion Sundays (nothing printed).
4. **Master (Lord) feasts**:
   - **Nativity, Epiphany, Transfiguration, Elevation (Sep 14) on Sunday: Resurrection service omitted entirely**; feast's kontakion only, no resurrectional at ode 3. Explicit: Typikon p.11 (list of days Resurrection hymns omitted), p.75-76 (Sep 14: "omit the hymns of the Resurrection"), p.144 (Dec 25 Sun), p.173-174 (Jan 6 Sun), p.449 (Aug 6 Sun: "nothing for the Resurrection or tone of the week").
   - **Presentation (Feb 2) on Sunday before the Triodion: shares like a Theotokos feast**: resurrectional at ode 3, Presentation kontakion at ode 6. Explicit p.219.
   - Circumcision/Basil (Jan 1) on Sunday: resurrectional at ode 3, **St Basil's kontakion at ode 6**. Explicit p.165.
5. **Forefeast / afterfeast / leavetaking Sundays**: resurrectional at ode 6 (no feast kontakion at Matins), explicit in Ch.V: Sep 7 forefeast p.64; Sep 9, 12 p.69-72; Sep 14 afterfeast p.80; Sep 21 Cross leavetaking p.84; Nov 14, 16, 20 (forefeast), 25 (leavetaking) pp.108-117; Jan 2-4 Sunday before Epiphany p.167. Exceptions, explicit:
   - **Dec 20-23 Sunday before Nativity: forefeast kontakion, not Forefathers/Genealogy** (p.135). Dec 18-19: Fathers (p.134). Dec 24: Fathers' kontakion+oikos read after ode 3 (p.136-137); the forefeast kontakion is also quoted there; ode-6 slot not isolated in text (key shows Forefathers, 2023-12-24).
   - **Jan 5 Sunday before Epiphany: forefeast kontakion** (p.169).
   - Mar 25 forefeast Sundays in Lent: forefeast kontakion (Ch.V pp.277-308, many variants; not sampled in key).
   - **Leavetaking of Dormition (Aug 23) on Sunday: Dormition kontakion** at ode 6, resurrectional at ode 3 (p.463-464). Differs from Cross leavetaking Sep 21 (resurrectional).
   - Aug 1 on Sunday (Cross procession): Cross kontakion at ode 3, resurrectional at ode 6 (p.444).
6. **Triodion Sundays**: Sunday's own kontakion (Publican/Prodigal/Last Judgment/Forgiveness/Lent 1/Lent 3). Lent 2, 4, 5: resurrectional. Annunciation on those: Ch.V pp.277-321 (not key-checked; Annunciation-on-Sunday in 2019-26 range falls only on Lent Sundays, all resurrectional per key).

## Conflicts / source discrepancies

- **C1 Typikon vs Violakis**: consistent. Violakis §26 names "Taxiarchs" for Archangels and phrases Sunday before/after Nativity instead of Genealogy/Sunday after; no substantive difference. Violakis §26 gives no Pentecostarion narrowing.
- **C2 Typikon p.28 vs Ch.V (Pentecostarion saints)**: p.28 says George/John/Constantine win on Pentecostarion Sundays (except Thomas, Pentecost, All Saints). Ch.V per-case: John on Myrrh-Bearers (p.365), Paralytic (p.368), Blind Man (p.376) -> **Sunday's own kontakion**; John on Fathers Sunday (p.381) -> **John**; Constantine & Helen on Samaritan (p.386) -> **Constantine**, on Blind Man (p.389) -> **Blind Man**, on Fathers (p.394/396) two variants; Pentecost/All Saints -> Sunday's. Key: 2022-05-08 (John on Myrrh-Bearers) = Myrrh-Bearers, 2023-05-21 (Constantine on Blind Man) = Blind Man: **follows Ch.V, not p.28**. Rule: Ch.V (specific) beats p.28 (general); George/John/Constantine do not override in general. Fathers-Sunday and Samaritan+Constantine cases remain Typikon-only (no key date in 2019-26). p.381/383/394/396 give paired variants without stating the condition: **missing** (which variant when).
- **C3 ANCA 2026 vs Typikon**: see below.

## Check against ANCA ORTHROS / Abbr

Method: ORTHROS PDFs (`servicetexts/<Mon DD YYYY> ORTHROS.pdf`, spelling variants per #9) for every Sunday 2019-01-06 .. 2026-11-22; 384 of 412 found (28 non-date filenames: Pascha cycle, Pentecost, feast-coincident). Spot-read Abbr for 2026-10-18, 11-08, 07-05, 07-12. Throwaway scripts, not committed.

Divergences (flag):
1. **ANCA 2026 prints two kontakia**, resurrectional first, then the other. From 2026-06-07 on: All Saints (06-07), Athanasius of Athos (07-05), Paisios (07-12), Fathers (07-19, 10-11), Dormition leavetaking (08-23), Luke (10-18), Archangels (11-08). Pre-2026 Luke (2020-10-18), Fathers, Archangels (2020), Dormition leavetaking (2020) printed only the second. Interpretation: 2026 prints the Typikon's ode-3 resurrectional + ode-6 feast pair (matches p.27 for Theotokos/listed saints/great saints; Violakis §25 "celebrated Saints"). **Luke, Athanasius, Paisios are not in p.28 list**; Ch.V Oct 18 (p.95) has resurrectional only. Abbr for 07-05/07-12 shows these saints have litia and doxastikon (great-saint treatment), so the p.27 sentence "on the feast of a great saint, we follow the same order" may justify them: **inferred, unstable across ANCA years**. Rule must not key on 2026 key for these. Hit also 2026-06-07: All Saints with resurrectional (p.28 gives All Saints' own only; Pentecostarion ode-3 sharing is unstated).
2. **ANCA 2026 afterfeast Sundays add the afterfeast kontakion (short, no oikos), before the resurrectional**: Cross afterfeast (2026-09-20), Entrance afterfeast (2026-11-22), Ascension afterfeast (2026-05-24, before Fathers). Typikon Ch.V (Sep 14 afterfeast p.80, Nov 21 p.115) and 2019-2025 key: resurrectional only. Candidate ANCA convention change, not a Typikon rule: treat as ANCA divergence for 2026, **inferred, non-Typikon**.

4. **ANCA-local commemoration**: Sunday of All Saints of Antioch (2025-06-22, 2026-06-14, also North America 2026-06-14): own kontakion. Not in Typikon. ANCA-specific, inferred.
5. **Master feasts on Sunday not testable**: Nativity-on-Sunday, Epiphany-on-Sunday, Elevation-on-Sunday (2025-09-14), Transfiguration-on-Sunday (2023-08-06) ORTHROS files use non-date filenames. ## Recommended Rule shape

`matins.kontakion` family, ordered, specific beats general (Ch.V per-date beats p.28):
1. Lord's-feast-on-Sunday (Nativity, Epiphany, Transfiguration, Elevation): feast only.
2. Triodion Sunday (not Lent 2/4/5), Pentecostarion Sunday, Forefathers/Genealogy/After-Nativity/Fathers/All Saints: Sunday's own.
3. Theotokos feast or leavetaking Aug 23: feast at 6; add `matins.kontakion.ode3` = resurrectional.
4. Presentation, Circumcision/Basil, listed saints, Ch.V-extension saints: saint at 6 + resurrectional at 3.
5. Forefeast table (Dec 20-23, Jan 5, Mar 25 Lent): forefeast kontakion; other forefeast/afterfeast: resurrectional.
6. Default resurrectional.
Treat ANCA 2026 "pair" and afterfeast additions as Divergence-log entries (class: ANCA anomaly/2026 convention), not Rules. ODE-3 resurrectional slot is an optional output slot (ANCA pre-2026 omits it).
