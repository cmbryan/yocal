# Other authoritative typika / liturgicons / rubric sources

Issue: cmbryan/yocal#11. Parent map: #5. Accessed 2026-10-29.
Public repo: nothing copied. Facts paraphrased; titles/URLs only. No PDFs committed.

## TL;DR

- **Best gap-filler for Antiochian: Bp Basil (Wichita) "Ranking of Feasts and Commemorations" + Parish Typikon Charts I-III.** Greek/Violakis-based, Antiochian-hosted, slot-by-slot tables for Sunday x feast-rank at Great Vespers, Orthros, Liturgy; festal seasons/leavetakings. Free PDFs. Licence unstated (assume all rights reserved).
- **Upstream of our #1 source:** the Antiochian Typikon (Khoury/Morris 2011) is a translation of Arman's 1951-52 Arabic Typikon, itself checked against the Typikon of the Great Church (Violakis, 1888). So Violakis = the root authority. Greek 1888 scans free (CC BY-NC-ND). English bilingual edition paywalled.
- **Russian/Sabaite cross-check:** Andreev draft English Typicon (CC BY-SA 3.0, selected chapters incl. Great Vespers + Sunday Matins); OCA liturgics tables (free to read, (c) OCA).
- **Not found:** standalone typikon/ordo from Archdiocese of Australia, Archdiocese of Thyateira, Holy Cross, Jordanville (free English). OCA has no standalone typikon, only tables.
- OCA tables explicitly do **not** cover a Great Feast on a Sunday (except Theotokos feasts). So they cannot fill the Major-feast-on-Sunday gap.

## Recommendation (gaps: Sat Great Vespers, Sun Matins/Liturgy, Major feasts)

| Priority | Source | Fills |

## Sources

Legend: Trad = tradition. Access: F = free online, P = paywalled/print.

### A. Antiochian

**A1. Parish Typikon Charts 1-3 + Ranking of Feasts and Commemorations (Bp Basil Essey, Wichita Chancery; 1st ed 1999, 2nd rev ed 2003)**
- Authority: Bp Basil (auxiliary bishop, Antiochian Archdiocese), hosted by Diocese of Wichita & Mid-America (St Raphael Clergy Brotherhood). Frames itself as a manual for ordering services correctly.
- Trad: Antiochian, applying Greek/Constantinople rules (cites Athonite usage in notes; Menaion listing from Apostoliki Diakonia, Athens 1993).
- Index: http://www.dowama.org/node/31 (also http://www.dowama.org/content/typikon)
- Docs (all under http://www.dowama.org/sites/docs/): `typicon_ranking_letter.pdf` (intro letter), `Descriptions of Ranks.pdf` (6pp), `Ranking_of_Feasts_according_to_the_Typikon.pdf` (14pp; per-month rank of each day, with sticheron counts e.g. "Octoechos x + Menaion y"), `RankingListOfFeastsAndCommemorations.pdf` (2pp), `Chart1.pdf` weekdays (Mon-Sat), `Chart2.pdf` Sundays (8pp), `Chart3.pdf` festal seasons and leavetakings (8pp).
- Coverage (verified by reading PDFs):
  - Chart II (printed pp.29-32+): columns by rank on a Sunday (5th simple, 4th doxology, 3rd vigil/polyeleos, 2nd Mother of God, 1st Master) x rows for Great Vespers, Orthros (Six Psalms, troparia, kathismata, poetic kathismata, polyeleos/amomos, evlogitaria...), Divine Liturgy (antiphons, entrance hymns, epistle/gospel). Gives Lord-I-Have-Cried Octoechos/Menaion split by class (e.g. 7+3 for simple down to 4+6 for Mother-of-God feast; Master feast = Menaion only), Glory/Both-now assignment, aposticha, apolytikia, dismissal, Litia/Artoklasia optionality. Footnotes list exceptions (e.g. Sept 26, Oct 26, Nov 8, Dec 6, Jan 30, June 24, June 29, 4th/7th Councils use 4+6 resurrection/commemoration; Three Hierarchs on Triodion Sundays).
  - Chart III: Great Vespers/Orthros when a festal season (forefeast/afterfeast) day or leavetaking coincides with a Sunday (e.g. 4+6 vs 6+4 split).
  - Ranking manual: class definitions; rule that kathisma 1 stays at Sat Great Vespers except Pentecost and Sunday coinciding with Nativity/Theophany/Transfiguration; feast of Master on Sunday = whole service for the feast; eve Great Vespers rules (Palm Sunday eve exception).
  - Gaps: Triodion/Pentecostarion Sunday specifics only via footnotes; no hymn text. Chart 1 weekdays out of scope.
- Access: F. Licence: none stated; site footer "Copyright 2003-2026 St. Raphael Clergy Brotherhood". Do not reproduce tables; cite chart + page.
- Trust: high for Antiochian-aligned practice (bishop-endorsed). Date: 2003; older than 2011 Typikon -> check for conflicts.

**A2. Antiochian Typikon "The Book of the Typikon" (Bp Demetri Khoury, tr.; Fr John Morris, ed.; Oct 2011)** (existing #1 source, noted for provenance)
- Translated from the Arabic edition by Rizkallah Arman (Beirut 1952; commissioned by Patriarch Alexander III 1951). Arman states he had Greek Typikon of the Great Church compared and added clarifying sections; Great Church usage derived from St Sabbas with changes.
- 583pp. Contents incl. ch. III Order of Sunday Services (p.46), Menaia by month, Triodion, Pentecostarion, ch. IX outlines (Great Vespers, Litia/Artoklasia, Matins with gospel).
- Public hosted copies: https://almoutran.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/typikon.pdf ; https://www.equip-orthodox.com/app/download/20479026/54-typikon-full.pdf . Encrypted (copy/edit disabled). No licence stated.

**A3. Antiochian Online Liturgical Guide / service texts** (antiochian.org `liturgicday`; #2 source in map). Already known; per search it supplies uniform texts, variable texts, readings, fasting rule per day. Not a rule source.

### B. Greek / Great Church (Constantinople)

**B1. Typikon of the Great Church of Christ, G. Violakis, Constantinople 1888 (Greek)**
- Authority: Ecumenical Patriarchate Patriarchal Press; Violakis (protopsaltes) revised and expanded Konstantinos's 1838 edition. Standard for Greek-speaking churches (Thyateira's Liturgics Seminar page says clergy serve per Typikon of the Great Church).
- Scan: https://digital.lib.auth.gr/record/125938 (Aristotle Univ Thessaloniki, Historical Library of Argyroupolis). 492pp (pp.481-482 missing). 5 PDFs (21-42 MB). Licence CC BY-NC-ND 3.0; "printing permitted". F.
- Other scans (less trusted): Analogion forum thread, psalticbookdroid blog, Scribd (not verified; licence unclear). Greek Protheoria (general principles): https://www.patirxristos.gr/ekklisiastiko-typiko/protheoria-g-violaki , https://lavaron.com.gr/protheoria-toy-typikoy-tis-toy-christoy/ (not fetched).
- Coverage: whole year (Menaion), Sunday vs feast coincidence, Triodion/Pentecostarion. Greek; scanned images (OCR not verified).
- Use: root authority to settle A1/A2 questions.

**B2. Violakis Typikon bilingual edition, Greek Orthodox Metropolis of Denver Church Music Federation (2015)**
- Tr. Fr Robert Athas; ed. George T. Demos; contrib. Theodore Bogdanos. 1155pp hardbound; stated identical to Violakis text, church dates converted to modern calendar, 170pp glossary. Brochure: $60 (2021). ISBN 978-0-692-24874-4 (per trisagionschool forum, secondary). P.
- Brochure: https://www.denver.churchmusic.goarch.org/assets/files/TYP-BBB%20BROCHURE%202021-10.pdf ; publishing page: https://denver.churchmusic.goarch.org/publishing/typikon-byzantine
- Trad: Greek (GOA-affiliated). Copyright: commercial; no free sample found. Practitioner comment (forum) says still hard to use.

**B3. Bogdanos, "The Byzantine Liturgy: Hymnology and Order" (Denver Fed.)** - perpetual Typikon of Divine Liturgy hymns for every Sunday/major feast (apolytikia, kontakia, entrance hymns, koinonika), 350pp, $38 (brochure above). P. Candidate check for Sunday Liturgy variables (Greek order).

**B4. greekorthodoxtypikon.blogspot.com** - English posts paraphrasing Violakis (Typika/Beatitudes on Sundays, apodosis of John's Nativity, etc.). Author unclear (profile "Joanna George"). Secondary; use only as pointer to Violakis passages. https://greekorthodoxtypikon.blogspot.com/

**B5. Annual Greek Typikon (Nektarios Panagopoulos, Univ of Athens page)** - http://users.uoa.gr/~nektar/fyllades/keimena/2022/typikon_2022.pdf. Host refused connection when fetched; unverified. Flag only.

**B6. Archdiocese of Thyateira (UK), Australia, Holy Cross/GOA.** Searches found no published ordo/rubric document. Thyateira: Liturgics Seminar page says service per Typikon of the Great Church (https://www.thyateira.org.uk/news/events/first-liturgics-seminar/). Australia: prayer book with Typica and bilingual Liturgy only. Holy Cross Orthodox Press: no typikon; handbook by Calivas (2023) on practice (not verified). So these = Violakis in practice, no separate rule source.

### C. Russian / Slavic (Sabaite Typikon)

**C1. A. Andreev, "The Typicon that is the Church Order of the Monastery of St. Sabbas near Jerusalem, according to the modern usage of the Russian Church" (English, DRAFT)**
- Ponomar Project. CC BY-SA 3.0 (c) Aleksandr Andreev. Draft, "not for use". Compared to print editions and Slavonic/Greek manuscripts; cites Nikolsky.
- Selected chapters (53pp, 2014 draft): https://www.ponomar.net/files/typicon_selected.pdf - covers order of Great Vespers and Sunday Matins, kathismata, readings, Sunday Matins rubrics.
- Patronal-feast chapters (124pp, March 2015): https://ponomar.net/files/temple_draft.pdf - Vigil-rank on Saturday/Sunday combos, Patronal feast coincidences.
- Index: https://www.ponomar.net/english.html
- F. CC BY-SA means copying text needs attribution + share-alike; citing/paraphrasing fine.
- Trad: Russian usage. Differs from Greek in places; use for divergence flagging, not authority for Antiochian.

**C2. Ponomar software (typiconman/ponomar, GPL-3.0)** - https://github.com/typiconman/ponomar . Java; README: alpha, test-only. Contains Matins reading-order logic (Matins.java), Liturgy, Hours; no Vespers module in tree. XML language packs. Prior art for rule encoding; not a rule source. Copyright 2006-2018 Andreev et al.

**C3. Nikolsky, Posobie k izucheniyu ustava bogosluzheniya (Russian; 1st pub 1862; 1907 ed. scans)** - standard Russian handbook. https://azbyka.ru/otechnik/Konstantin_Nikolskij/posobie-k-izucheniyu-ustava-bogosluzhenija-pravoslavnoj-tserkvi/ (1995 reprint text; EPUB/PDF); Commons djvu 1907 scan. Nikolsky 1824-1910 (public-domain age; azbyka reprint terms not stated). F. Russian language. TOC (as seen) does not show an explicit Sunday x feast coincidence chapter.

**C4. Skaballanovich, "Tolkovyj Tipikon" (Russian, explanatory Typikon with history)** - https://azbyka.ru/otechnik/Mihail_Skaballanovich/tolkovyj-tipikon/ (2003 Palomnik ed.). F. Russian. Terms not stated. Not read in depth.

**C5. Sokolof, "A Manual of the Orthodox Church's Divine Services" (transl. Albany NY 1899)** - overview (192pp modern reprint), not a typikon; has section on feast-day and fast-time services. PD scan: https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/manualoforthodox0000arch ; CCEL: https://ccel.org/ccel/sokolof/services ; reprint Holy Trinity Publications 2023 (P). Low value for coincidence rules.

**C6. Fekula & Williams, "The Order of Divine Services" (St John of Kronstadt Press, 1997; Russian usage)** - excerpts (incl. Sunday services) at http://www.holytrinitymission.org/books/english/order_divine_services_fecula.htm ; print P. Hosting site provenance unclear. Medium trust.

**C7. holytrinitymission.org "Typikon, Part 1"** - http://www.holytrinitymission.org/books/english/typikon_1.htm - compiled explanatory text (Russian usage; cites Nikolsky pages). Compiler unattributed. Low/medium trust.

**C8. orthodox.net/ustav (St Nicholas, McKinney TX)** - Russian Typicon notes, Sunday kathisma Qs, Bulgakov Handbook link, rank of feasts. https://www.orthodox.net/ustav/ . (c) all rights reserved. F. Secondary.

**C9. St John (Maximovitch), "How to Keep the Church Typicon" (ROCOR 1951)** - principle: keep core structure and the day's commemorative elements when abbreviating. Not a rule table. https://www.orthodoxinfo.com/praxis/typicon.aspx (c) OCIC.

**C10. saintjonah.org rubrics (parish site)** - saintjonah.org vigil rubrics (weekly, Old Calendar, 2026 vigils), "all rights reserved". https://saintjonah.org/rub/ . Per-date rubrics as worked examples of Russian practice, not general rules. Not reviewed in detail.

**C11. Bulgakov "Handbook for Church Servers"** (Russian, 1913; English transl. hosted at http://www.transfigcathedral.org/faith/Bulgakov/index.shtml per orthodox.net). Not fetched; unverified.

### D. OCA

**D1. OCA Liturgics outlines/tables** - https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines (index). Relevant: General Note https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines/general-note-regarding-the-typicon ; Classes of Feasts .../classes-of-feasts ; Vespers tables outside Lent-Pascha .../vespers-tables-of-variable-parts-outside-lent-pascha ; Kathisma readings tables.
- Models, not a named typikon edition; says it reconciles several practices; local authority may modify. Explicitly excludes Great Feast x Sunday except Theotokos feasts (incl. Circumcision, Peter & Paul, John's Nativity/Beheading counted as "Great Feasts" there).
- Vespers table: Sunday Great Vespers by saint rank (lesser, polyeleos, vigil/temple), forefeast/afterfeast variants, Theotokos feast on Sunday, leavetaking x Sunday. Pentecostarion part marked incomplete.
- (c) OCA 1996-2026, all rights reserved. F to read. Trad: OCA (Russian-derived).

### E. Western-hosted Byzantine Catholic (structural cross-check only)

**E1. Metropolitan Cantor Institute: Common Typikon + Annual Typikon + "Setting the Services" (Fr David Petras, Byzantine Catholic Archeparchy of Pittsburgh; Byzantine Seminary Press)** - https://mci.archpitt.org/setting/Common_Typikon.html ; https://mci.archpitt.org/setting/index.html . "Formats" 14-20 Sundays with saints/Theotokos feasts/apodosis days; 21-23 Triodion; 24-26 Paschal season (per page). Annual Typikon uses Gregorian calendar and Western Paschalion. Print loose-leaf P (~100pp); web pages F. Licence not stated. Not Orthodox; useful as a worked structure.

### F. English books (paywalled, copyrighted)

- **The Festal Menaion**, tr. Mother Mary & Archimandrite Kallistos Ware (Faber 1969, 564pp). Variable parts and detailed rubrics for the nine fixed-calendar Great Feasts + intro to service structure (per Metropolitan Cantor Institute and publisher listings; https://mci.archpitt.org/liturgy/Menaion.html ; Jordanville: https://churchsupplies.jordanville.org/products/festal-menaion ). P. Russian-influenced Orthodox. A Lenten Triodion PDF appears on ponomar.net/data/lenten_triodion.pdf; licence unclear, do not rely on it.
- **Job Getcha, The Typikon Decoded** (SVS Press, 313pp, ISBN 978-088141-412-7). Required text in Antiochian House of Studies liturgics course (per search). Publisher page lists coverage only generally ("offices throughout the Byzantine liturgical year", historical development, practical use); I could not confirm Sunday/feast detail. https://svspress.com/the-typikon-decoded-an-explanation-of-byzantine-liturgical-practice . P.
- Mother Mary/Ware Triodion and HTM Pentecostarion: movable-feast variable text (needed for Triodion Sundays) - not fetched.
