# Typikon Sunday-Vespers stichera count audit

Issue #43 (map #5; follows #42 tiebreak, #39 policy). Paraphrased; no PDFs/long excerpts. Throwaway branch, not for merge.

## 0. Conventions

- "p.NN" = printed footer page, Antiochian Typikon (Khoury/Morris 2011; PDF page - 3). "Vio. §N" = Violakis 1888 Protheoria section (`Violakis/translation.md`, transcribed as printed, not emended). ANCA = AOCANA `servicetexts` Great Vespers (Sat eve) and Orthros PDFs, located via `prototypes/anca-index/pages.json`; Vespers dates = the Saturday file date (eve of the Sunday); Orthros/praises dates = the Sunday. BpB (`BpBasil/`) not needed; no verdict rests on it.
- R = Resurrection stichera. "LIHC" = "Lord, I have cried". Count notation `N: aR+b+c` = declared total N, parts by source.
- Verdicts (#39 scheme): **Typikon holds** / **ANCA holds** / **misreading**; plus `open` where evidence too thin (1 ANCA sample). Conflicts recorded `open` / `accepted` / `misreading`.
- Method: `pdftotext -layout Typikon.pdf`; every "O Lord I have cried ... we chant N stichera" block parsed for declared N vs summed parts; every block whose parts include Resurrection stichera kept (171 Vespers rows + 2 manual; includes a few weekday Pascha-cycle rows, marked out of scope). Praises counts matched to each Vespers block. ANCA: 46 Sat-eve VESP files + 26 Orthros files fetched/parsed (verse 10..1 grouped by heading).

## 1. Pattern reference (what "expected" means)

Typikon p.12 (Ch.I): Sunday between a Theotokos feast and its leavetaking = 6R+4; great saint on a Sunday after a Lord feast or on a Theotokos leavetaking = 4R+3+3; **4R+6 list** = Archangels, Nativity of Forerunner, Peter and Paul, John Evangelist (Sep 26), Three Hierarchs (not on a Triodion Sunday), Fathers of the 4th and 7th Councils, Demetrius, Nicholas; saint without Doxastikon = 7R+3 (pp.12, 48). Vio. §7 pp.6-7 says the same (4R+6 on principal days *and apodoses* of Theotokos feasts; 6R+4 afterfeast Sundays and celebrated saints; 4R+3+3 saint on afterfeast/apodosis; 7R+3 no Doxastikon; R omitted on Nativity, Theophany, Palm Sun, Thomas Sun, Pentecost, Transfiguration, Exaltation). Vio. §31 pp.24-25 (praises): Theotokos feast/apodosis/afterfeast 4R+4; saint + afterfeast/apodosis 3R+2+3; Meeting on Meatfare/Cheesefare 3R+2 Triodion+3; Annunciation Lent 3 = 2R+3 Cross+3, Lent 4/5 = 4R+4.

**Correction to the ticket's pattern list:** a *leavetaking* (apodosis) on a Sunday is 4R+6 (Vio. §7; Typikon pp.71, 84, 190, 452, 463), not 6R+4. 6R+4 is for afterfeast Sundays (between feast and leavetaking) and saints with a Glory. Treat "afterfeast/leavetaking 6R+4" as wrong when reading the table.

### 2.2 Notes

- **Typikon-internal 7R+3 with Glory** (rows 6, 7): p.12/48 reserve 7R+3 for a saint *without* a Doxastikon; pp.168/174/421 print 7R+3 and also list a Glory. Either the 7R+3 or the Glory line is off; ANCA sides with the Glory (6R+4). Provisional: low sample.
- **Triodion/Pentecostarion 7R+3 rows** (pp.470, 527, 529, 533): plain Sunday-of frames, not saint cases; no contradiction.
- **Praises on 4R+3+3 days:** Typikon prints 4R+4 (saint or second commemoration only: pp.82, 117, 121, 155, 160, 466) where Vio. §31 prescribes 3R+2+3. ANCA follows the Typikon (2019-09-01 Indiction+Simeon 4R+4; 2019-12-29, 2020-12-27, 2023-01-01 4R+4). Typikon holds; Vio. dissent = class-scoped Violakis divergence, not a Source conflict. Two-saint Sundays print 2R+3+3 (pp.129, 131; ANCA 2024-10-13 Fathers+Jacob 2R+3+3 matches).
- **Leavetaking-on-Sunday rows are consistent:** pp.71, 84, 190, 452, 463 all 4R+6; ANCA 2021-09-11 (Nativity Theotokos), 2025-09-20 (Cross), 2023-08-12 (Transfiguration) 4R+6. Only Dormition (row 8) diverges. p.190 (Epiphany, Jan 14 Sun) unsampled: 2024-01-13 ANCA page lists no files.
- **Dec 31 Sunday + Nativity leavetaking (pp.157, 161)** print 3R+4+3 (R reduced). Unusual but ANCA 2023-12-30 matches: Typikon holds.
- **Miracle of Archangel Michael (Sep 6, p.62) 6R+4** is not a p.12 list violation (list = Synaxis Nov 8, p.101 4R+6, ANCA 2026-11-08 4R+6); ANCA 2020-09-05 6R+4.
- **Arithmetic:** only p.289 (declared 9 vs 10) is a true declared-total slip among 170+ LIHC blocks. All other declared vs parts differences were parser artifacts (page-break splits, "repeating the first to make N" wording) and were checked by hand: pp.60, 68, 165, 382, 392, 401, 413, 457 sum to 10.
- **Pentecostarion/Bright Week 8-stichera and atypical combos** (pp.322, 325, 349, 326, 319, 280, 281, 380, 543, 546): self-consistent; no ANCA/Violakis sample, left `unverified`.

### 2.3 Recommended Rule changes

- Presentation Sun (p.218) and Annunciation Lent 5 (p.305): Rule 4R+6, log Typikon as dissent (#42).
- Fathers of 7th Council and Three Hierarchs on pre-Triodion Sun: Rule 4R+6 (p.12 list wins over pp.93, 212).
- Fathers of 4th Council: Rule 6R+4 (p.433, ANCA 5/5); p.12 list and Vio. dissent.
- Jan 5 Sun before Epiphany and Jun 30 12 Apostles: Rule 6R+4 with Glory, provisional; log p.168/174/421 as Source conflict `open`.
- Dormition leavetaking on Sun: Rule 4R+6 (Typikon p.463, Vio.); log ANCA 2021 divergence `open`.
- Pentecostarion Myrrhbearers + saint (p.332/354/359/364): Rule per Typikon 4R+2+4, Source conflict `open` (ANCA 2022 4R+3+3).
- p.289 "9" -> 10; p.205 praises "2 for St Gregory" -> 3; p.227 praises 10 -> 3R+2+3; p.490 praises 9 -> 8 (4R+4).
- Reading rule for the parser: count `total of N`/`repeating` wording before checking sums; do not split counts across the Typikon page break (p.220/221).

## 4. Caveats

- ANCA samples are thin for several rows (1 each: Dormition leavetaking, 12 Apostles, Myrrhbearers+saint, Annunciation Lent 5 (#42), Holy Sat (#42)). Verdicts marked provisional/`open` there.
- Parser reads declared counts from the pdftotext layout; Matins praises matched by proximity. Heading context is best-effort; check the Typikon page for exact conditions.
- Ch.V is Menaion-ordered; Triodion/Pentecostarion pages carry no dates. Some "If the feast falls ..." conditions (e.g. Jan 25 on Meatfare Sunday) cannot occur in the Gregorian cycle 2018-26 and so have no ANCA evidence.
- No independent Greek/Slavonic Typikon print checked for the p.12-list contradictions. BpB not used.
- Violakis transcribed as printed; where it disagrees with Typikon+ANCA (row 5, praises on 4R+3+3 days) it is reported as dissent, not emended.
